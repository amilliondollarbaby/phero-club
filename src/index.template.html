Phero comes from pheromones, the invisible pull that draws people together. We can't bottle that. But we can make the scents that set the mood for it.

phe·ro
/ˈfɛr.oʊ/ · say it FAIR-oh
  1. Short for pheromone: the scent of attraction.
  2. Hand-poured dessert candles, made by two friends in the Philippines.
  3. A club. You're a member the moment you open the box.
The name is our inspiration, not an ingredient. Our candles don't contain pheromones.
